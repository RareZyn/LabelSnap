# LabelSnap — Design Brief

**Name:** LabelSnap (one word, camel-case L and S)
**What it is:** A progressive web app for researchers. Snap a label, product or lab photo; it auto-detects the
quadrilateral, rectifies and crops it, reads the text with on-device OCR, and drops the capture into a formatted
document as a numbered figure with a caption and footnote.
**Audience:** Researchers and lab staff. Technical, precise, impatient with repetitive admin. Phone in one hand,
sample in the other, often with no signal.
**Adjectives:** futuristic, precise, fast, field-ready, lab-grade.
**Must work:** as a PWA app icon and as a 16 px favicon (FR-118, NFR-18). Phone-first, one-handed.
**Palette (fixed):** Raven Black `#080402`, Powder Blue `#B6DFF5`, Poppy Red `#E0091F`.
**Constraint from the spec:** Poppy Red is only ~4.1:1 on Raven Black, so red is for fills, icons and large
shapes — never small text. A logo is a shape, not body text, so red is usable here as mass.

## Category conventions (what to avoid)

From the library: productivity/collaboration is 38% blue or cyan, 27% containment, 25% circle geometry.
Design/creative is 29% containment. The default mark in this category is **a blue rounded square with a white
glyph inside** — and Powder Blue would walk straight into it.

**Ruled out as clichés:**
- Blue rounded-square app tile with a centred white glyph
- Camera body with a lens circle (Instagram owns the reduced-camera glyph)
- Document page with a folded corner; a stack of paper
- Magnifying glass for OCR or search
- Upward arrow or swoosh for "productivity"
- An "A" or "T" in a box for text recognition

## Word map

| Source | Words |
| ---- | ---- |
| Name | **Snap** = shutter click *and* snap-into-place. **Label** = the subject, and the caption. L is the initial. |
| Offering | detect, rectify, crop, read, place, number, caption |
| Signature interaction | the four draggable corner handles on a detected quad (FR-057, FR-060) |
| Nouns | crop bracket, corner handle, quadrilateral, focus frame, figure slot, caption line, grid, shutter |
| Metaphors | four corners closing on a subject; a crooked thing snapping straight; a capture landing in its slot |
| Opposites | manual transfer, a loose pile of photos, skew |

**Intersections circled:** *corner bracket* sits at the junction of the name (L is a corner), the interaction
(corner handles), the camera language (focus brackets) and small-size strength (big separated shapes).

**The happy accident this identity is built on:** the letter **L is already a crop corner**. The brand's initial
and the product's core gesture are the same shape.
